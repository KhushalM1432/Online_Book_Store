## Online Bookstore Sales, Customer & Inventory Analytics | SQL

## Data Science

### **MySQL | SQL | Relational Database Design | Revenue Analytics | Customer Analytics | Inventory Management**

## Project Overview

Developed a SQL-based Online Bookstore Database Analytics project using MySQL to analyze book sales, customer purchasing behavior, revenue performance, and inventory availability.

The project demonstrates practical relational database design, SQL querying, data aggregation, and business analysis techniques to transform transactional records into meaningful insights for bookstore operations.

## Business Problem

Online bookstores need reliable data to monitor revenue, identify popular books, understand customer purchasing behavior, and manage available inventory.

This project addresses these challenges by using SQL queries to analyze sales transactions, customer activity, book categories, and stock availability.

## Project Objectives

- Design and work with a structured relational database.
- Analyze bookstore sales and revenue performance.
- Identify high-performing books, authors, and genres.
- Examine customer purchasing frequency and spending patterns.
- Monitor book inventory and remaining stock.
- Generate business insights through SQL queries.

## Database Structure

The project uses three primary tables:

### 1. Books

Stores book information, including book identifiers, titles, authors, genres, prices, and stock availability.

### 2. Customers

Contains customer information used to analyze purchasing activity and customer distribution.

### 3. Orders

Stores customer order transactions, including purchased books, quantities, and transaction values.

## Project Highlights

1. **Relational Database Design:** Developed a structured MySQL database connecting books, customers, and orders to support integrated business analysis.

2. **Data Extraction & Filtering:** Applied `SELECT`, `WHERE`, `BETWEEN`, `ORDER BY`, and `LIMIT` to retrieve business records and investigate sales transactions.

3. **Revenue & Sales Performance Analysis:** Used `SUM()`, `AVG()`, and `COUNT()` to calculate revenue-related metrics and summarize transaction activity.

4. **Customer Purchasing Behavior Analysis:** Examined customer order frequency, spending patterns, and purchasing activity to understand customer engagement.

5. **Book Genre & Author Analysis:** Applied `GROUP BY` and `HAVING` to compare book categories and evaluate author-level sales performance.

6. **Relational Data Integration:** Used `INNER JOIN` and `LEFT JOIN` to combine book, customer, and order records for cross-table business analysis.

7. **High-Value Customer Identification:** Investigated customer spending and repeat-order patterns to identify commercially valuable customer groups.

8. **Inventory & Stock Analysis:** Analyzed book quantities and remaining stock to support inventory monitoring and replenishment planning.

9. **SQL-Based Business Reporting:** Organized analytical queries to answer practical questions about revenue, customers, product performance, and inventory.

10. **Business Insights & Decision Support:** Connected SQL query results with business objectives involving sales performance, customer engagement, and inventory planning.
